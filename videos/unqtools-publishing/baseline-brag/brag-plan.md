# Brag Plan: UnQTools (baseline 20s launch brag)

Baseline concept generated with the vendored /brag skill (`brag/skills/brag/SKILL.md`).
The extended 60s and 180s films reuse this researched identity and angle.

## What is this app?
UnQTools is a 100% static, privacy-first PWA with 1,679 free browser tools — converters,
calculators, generators, formatters, PDF utilities, SEO tools — that all run entirely in
the browser: no uploads, no accounts, no tracking.

## The angle
"The right tool shouldn't live in twelve tabs." Every demo beat shows a real UnQTools
tool doing its job (JSON formatted, password rolled, QR drawn, PDF compressed) inside the
product's own charcoal/terracotta design language — precision-engineered, quiet confidence.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Three mono task lines slam in one by one — "compress a pdf." / "format json." /
"shrink an image." — then a terracotta slash wipes them into one word: "or one tab."

## Key moments (the middle)
- JSON Formatter: minified sample → press Format → clean indented output (real sample from the tool source).
- Password Generator: length slider → generate → strong password with green strength bar.
- QR Code Generator: URL chip → real QR code of unqtools.pages.dev snaps in.

## Outro / punchline
Logo lockup + "unqtools.pages.dev" + "1,679 tools. Free. Private."

## User flow worth showing
Entry (search/task) → pick a tool → input → action → result. Baseline shows the action→result beats;
the extended films add the entry/search beats.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: "premium product launch film — precision engineering, warm restraint"
- Interpretation: confident holds, smooth power3 motion, sparse tasteful SFX; no chaotic cuts.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #262624 (dark charcoal, real `--background` dark token)
- Accent: #d97757 (terracotta/copper, real `--primary` dark token), deep #b5562d
- Text: #faf9f5 (warm cream), muted #b7b5a9, borders #3e3e38, panel #30302e
- Secondary: lavender→pink gradient headline treatment seen in the real hero; green #34d399 for "runs in your browser" chips
- Display font: Geist (variable, local woff2)
- Body font: Geist / Geist Mono for labels, bytes, code
- Strongest visual element: rounded-square terracotta logo tile + "UnQTools" wordmark; pill badges; mono byte counters

## Share copy (draft)
UnQTools: 1,679 private browser tools — PDF, image, dev, and more — that never leave your device. One tab, zero uploads.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed + sparse professional accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10 (1:00, bundled, trimmed to 20s with fades)
- Music treatment: fade in 0–0.6s, full bed under highlights, fade out over final logo
- Music cue guidance: preset cues read from bundled JSON; strongCues 3.55/4.10 (logo land, beat-locked 4.10) and 11.46 (QR snap); beat-grid ~0.55s spacing for badge/card sequences
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — hero glow and card presence breathe with RMS; no waveform visuals
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched (button clicks on presses, soft impact on logo, card sounds on sequences)
- Restraint rule: never more than one SFX per beat; no bright/hissy repeats

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Twelve tabs — 3.0s
Mono lines slam in: "compress a pdf." (0.15–1.0), "format json." (1.0–1.85), "shrink an image." (1.85–2.7),
terracotta slash wipe 2.7–3.0 reveals "or one tab."
Sequential/interaction: yes — three mono lines one by one with key ticks.
Audio intent: dry rhythmic ticks, tension.
Music: bed fades in.
Transition mood: hard wipe → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Brand reveal — 4.0s (3.0–7.0)
Logo tile drops at 4.10 (beat-locked strong cue), wordmark "UnQTools", tagline
"Private tools that respect you" (verbatim site hero).
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: soft impact on logo land; bed swells.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Tools doing work — 8.0s (7.0–15.0)
Three product cards, 2.7s each, beat-grid entrances:
a) JSON Formatter mini-UI: minified line → Format press → indented output.
b) Password Generator: slider tick → generate → password rolls, green "Strong" bar.
c) QR Code: URL chip unqtools.pages.dev → real QR snaps in (strong cue 11.46 ≈ card c land).
Sequential/interaction: yes — three cards in sequence; simulated button presses.
Audio intent: click on each press, card sound on each arrival.
Transition mood: clean wipes → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Privacy — 3.0s (15.0–18.0)
"100% in your browser" headline; four pill badges pop on alternating beats:
No uploads · No accounts · No tracking · Works offline (all verified site claims).
Sequential/interaction: yes — badges one by one, each held ≥0.8s on screen (fast-in then hold).
Audio intent: one soft tick per badge.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro — 2.0s (18.0–20.0)
Logo lockup + unqtools.pages.dev + "1,679 tools. Free. Private."
Audio intent: bed fades; one warm impact rings over.
Transition mood: settle to black-adjacent charcoal.

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat-professional, restrained
